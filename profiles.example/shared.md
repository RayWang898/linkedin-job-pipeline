## Background
- Name, current location, work authorization (for example "authorized to work in the US").
- Education, in one line each.
- Years of experience and the industries you have worked in.

## Shared experience
List the projects and roles you would put on any resume. For each one, write what you did and one number that shows the result.
- Example: Ran a 6-person campaign team for a product launch; delivered 12 events and 40+ media placements in 8 months.

## Skills
Tools, software and languages you can back up in an interview. List only what you can do.

## Job types worth a custom resume (needs_custom)
Describe job types that fit you but are not fully covered by any of your base resumes. A job in one of these types gets flagged "needs custom resume".
- Example: Roles in healthcare communications, where the base resumes do not show regulated-content experience.

## Hard rules
Things that make a job a bad fit no matter what the score is.
- Example: no roles that require frequent travel.
